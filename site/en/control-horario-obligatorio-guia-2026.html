Mandatory time tracking in 2026: what companies must do

Published on March 31, 2026 by TrustCore S.L.

Workday registration is not only a legal requirement. It also gives visibility into workload, timing, and productivity. With a simple system, compliance and execution can move together.

Practical requirements you cannot skip

Record start and end of workday, keep historical logs, and provide evidence when requested. The system must also be reliable for all shift types and schedules.

How to roll it out without friction

Start with a short policy, clear clock-in channels, and team communication. Then automate reports and periodic reviews to avoid last-minute manual tasks.

Indicators that help you improve

Track clock-in incidents, overtime, and validation delays. These indicators help correct process issues before they become labor conflicts or compliance risks.

Common operational mistakes

Allowing unlogged exceptions or skipping weekly inconsistency reviews creates administrative noise. Effective time tracking depends on simple rules and consistent follow-up.
